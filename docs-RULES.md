# Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026 — Rules Summary
Source: start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com (/rules, /resources), read 2026-10-21.

## Dates (WAT = UTC+1)
- Entry opens: Sep 24 2026 10:00 PT (PDT) = **Sep 24 2026 18:00 WAT**
- **Submission deadline: Nov 18 2026 12:00 PM PT (PST, UTC-8) = Nov 18 2026 21:00 WAT**
- Judging ~Nov 18 – Dec 9 2026; winners ~Dec 11 2026.

## Eligibility (Nigeria, 18+)
- 18+ and age of majority; not resident of Brazil, Quebec or sanctioned areas (Nigeria is not excluded — but "countries where banking/wire restrictions apply" are excluded; confirm you can receive US wire/payments).
- REQUIRED: active Meta account with **Developer Access**, and **membership in the Meta VR Start Program** by submission time. Devpost account + "Join Hackathon".
- One entry per individual. No prior Meta funding for the project.

## Platform
- Must be a VR/MR app **for Meta VR devices (Quest / Horizon OS)**. Ray-Ban glasses / Android XR are NOT listed.
- Allowed builds: Unity, Unreal, Godot, Native/Android → APK in Meta VR Developer Dashboard, release channel named **"Competition"**; or **IWSDK (WebXR)** → public URL (GitHub Pages, Vercel etc. allowed).
- Tools must be publicly available / available to Start members. No unreleased Meta SDKs. (IWSDK = Meta Immersive Web SDK; R3F/three.js WebXR is a web stack — IWSDK is the named path, consider adopting it.)
- No store/App Lab publication required; app must stay free & accessible until winner announcement.

## Requirements
- **Hands-first** (fully usable without controllers), seated-friendly (2 ft radius), fast in/out (≤10 min), original (not a thin wrapper around a 3rd-party AI).
- English (or English subtitles). No brand logos/ads, no identifiable real persons, content guidelines 10+/13+/18+.
- Tracks: Entertainment, Gaming, **Productivity** (FixLine fits). Divisions: New Experience (built after Sep 24 2026) or Adapted/Significantly Updated.
- Submission: project link, **demo video < 3 min** on YouTube/Vimeo (public) showing Quest/XR-simulator footage (no AI-generated video carrying the pitch), text description (inspiration, build, future plans, target launch date), track + division.

## Judging (Stage 1 pass/fail viability, then 4 × 25%)
Innovation & Creativity; Experience Design (purposeful passthrough, FoV-aware UI, onboarding); Technical Implementation (hand tracking, passthrough, scene understanding/anchors, ≥60 fps on Quest); Polish & Presentation.

## Prizes (~$1M)
Per track × division: Entertainment & Gaming $100k / $50k runner-up; **Productivity $70k / $35k**. Specials $25k each: Social & Multiplayer, **Best Agentic Interaction** (good fit), Reason to Come Back, First Five Minutes, **Accessibility Forward**, Boldest Original Concept; Judge's Choice 2×$20k. Non-US winners: US tax withholding possible (Form 1042-S); bank account needed.

## Resources / credits
- Resources page lists docs only (MQDH, Immersive Web Emulator, IWSDK deploy via GitHub Pages/Vercel, Interaction SDK, MRUK, agentic "Skills"). **No free credits, hardware or API access are offered** on the rules/resources pages. Any extras would come via the Meta VR Start Program itself (apply/join there).
